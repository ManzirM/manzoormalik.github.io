ABOUT

About Me

I am an applied health researcher interested in economic evaluation, population health, health inequalities, multiple long-term conditions (MLTCs), and the use of linked administrative data to understand and improve health and wellbeing.

My Research

My current research focuses on how socio-economic and health-related interventions affect health, wellbeing, and inequalities, including work on household support programmes and multiple long-term conditions using linked electronic health records, longitudinal survey data, and other healthcare datasets.

I am particularly interested in using linked data in natural policy experiment settings to understand how public and social policy interventions influence health outcomes and inequalities across different population groups.

Current Position

I am currently a Research Associate in Health Economics within the Health Economics and Equity (HEEL) Group at the University of Liverpool.

I will be joining Durham University as an Assistant Professor in early November this year
